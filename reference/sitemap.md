## Page-by-Page SEO & Conversion Strategy

### Core Pages
- **/ (Home)** — Target: "Commercial heat pumps Australia", "Industrial heat pumps". Goal: establish engineering authority, display GBP reviews/trust badges, drive traffic to Free ROI Assessment.
- **/free-roi-assessment** — Conversion page. Multi-step lead capture form (current fuel source? monthly bill? industry? contact info). No distractions or exit links.
- **/about-us** — Target: "Commercial HVAC engineers Australia". Goal: engineering background, local Victorian expertise, industry partnerships.
